# Community conduct

Keep discussions respectful, constructive and focused on the work. Explain
technical disagreements with evidence, make room for different experience
levels, and help people correct mistakes without personal attacks.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats and deliberate disclosure of another
person's private information are not acceptable. Keep credentials, tenant
details and customer data out of discussions and contributions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive
discussions or restrict participation to keep the project useful and welcoming.
To raise a conduct concern privately, use the
[official Selectec contact page](https://selectec.com/contact/) and ask for the
repository maintainers. Share only the details needed to explain the concern.
